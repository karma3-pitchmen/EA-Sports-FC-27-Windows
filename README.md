## 🕹️ About

EA SPORTS FC 27 is a football simulation game developed by EA Canada and EA Romania and published by Electronic Arts. Build and manage teams, compete across authentic leagues and competitions, and play fluid matches with enhanced attacking awareness, dynamic corners, and rebalanced defending. The pre-release launches worldwide on September 25, 2026.

Built for football fans who want an authentic sports simulation with competitive matches, team management, progression, and social multiplayer.

## ✨ Features

- ⚽ **True-to-Football Gameplay** — Control fluid matches with improved attacking awareness, dynamic corners, and player-focused defending.
- 🌆 **The Grounds** — Explore a social football playground featuring kickabouts, 1v1 matches, progression, and Clubs competition.
- 🧑‍💼 **Manager Career** — Build your club through a rebuilt transfer market with dynamic values, bidding, form, and negotiation tactics.
- 🏆 **Football Ultimate Team** — Create and develop a dream squad while collecting Player Items and competing throughout the season.
- 🖼️ **FUT Gallery** — Curate Player Items into collections, increase your Gallery Level, and earn rewards.
- 🌍 **Authentic Football** — Play with thousands of players, hundreds of clubs and national teams, major leagues, and renowned competitions.
- 🎮 **Competitive Multiplayer** — Compete through online PvP, online co-op, local multiplayer, and shared-screen modes.
